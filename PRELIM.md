# Preliminary results

Replace the prompts. The plot and the table live in `analysis.ipynb` and in `fig/`.

## Data in hand

One sentence on what was loaded. Point to the raw-data plot and the summary table.

## Split

What was held out, and why that split matches the decision. When the decision is about a later time, hold out a later time.

## Baseline

The simple method, and its held-out score in the units of the decision.

## Model

The method you fit, and its held-out score in the same units. Say where it beats the baseline and where it ties.

## Loss

The loss or the profit as a formula. Say which mistake costs more.

## Still open

What the final checkpoint still has to add. Uncertainty and a calibration check belong here if you do not have them yet.
